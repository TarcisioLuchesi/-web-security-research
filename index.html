Philosophy

Learn by building, use AI to expand my capabilities, understand through reverse engineering, and turn to theory when needed.

My learning is driven by projects and real-world problems. Rather than waiting to master all the theory, I prefer to build, test, analyze outcomes, and deepen my understanding on demand. AI accelerates this process by helping generate hypotheses, analyze code, draft materials, automate tasks, and create PoCs, but every result must be understood and validated.

In cybersecurity, I do not just want to chase bounties; I want to understand vulnerabilities, produce useful research, and become highly skilled in a specific niche.

For me, CVEs, exploits, write-ups, tools, and hands-on research are also ways to learn. The more I investigate a vulnerability, the more theory stops being abstract and becomes part of real work.

I also believe security knowledge should be shared. Sharing methodologies, explaining patterns, and helping others learn increases the number of people who can research and protect systems.

About

My current focus is WordPress vulnerability research. I usually start with the attack surface exposed by a plugin, trace data flow to a sensitive sink, and then validate the hypothesis in a lab.

This site is a place to organize my studies and publications. The PoCs and tests described here are intended for my own environments or authorized testing.
